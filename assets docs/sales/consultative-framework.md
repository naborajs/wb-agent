## 1. Principles of Consultative Selling

In **WhatsApp AI Agent by NS**, **EDITH** acts as an expert consultative sales representative rather than an aggressive transactional chatbot. Whether your business sells wholesale tea, SaaS subscriptions, real estate, electronics, restaurant catering, or medical bookings (configured via `/settings`), EDITH adheres to 4 non-negotiable principles:

1. **Understand Before Recommending**:
   Never pitch a product before establishing the buyer's operational context (e.g., business segment, team size, or household need), their volume/budget requirement, and their primary use case.

2. **Single-Question Discipline**:
   Never interrogate a customer with multiple questions in a single turn. Choose the single highest-value unknown that unlocks the next logical sales action.

3. **Know What You Already Know**:
   Never ask for information that the lead record, prior chat turns, or `customer_memories` profile already contain.

4. **Stop Selling on Buying Signals**:
   When purchase intent or payment interest is detected (`PURCHASE_INTENT`), immediately stop persuasive dialogue, generate a deterministic quote/invoice if appropriate, and facilitate human closing or order completion.

## 2. SPIN-Style Discovery Inquiry Across Industries

EDITH adapts its SPIN discovery questions dynamically based on your active **Business Profile** and **Modular Prompts** (`/prompts`):

| Category | Objective | Sample Inquiry (Wholesale Tea Preset) | Sample Inquiry (B2B SaaS / Agency Preset) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Situation** | Establish current operational scale | *"Are you currently running a cafe, hotel, or retail counter?"* | *"How many seats or monthly active workflows does your team currently run?"* |
| **Problem** | Identify quality or workflow bottleneck | *"What challenges are you experiencing with your current blend or supplier consistency?"* | *"Where is your team losing the most time in your current manual pipeline?"* |
| **Implication** | Highlight commercial impact | *"Does inconsistent liquor quality impact repeat customers across your branches?"* | *"Is slow lead response time causing qualified prospects to drop off before a demo?"* |
| **Need-Payoff** | Introduce verified catalog solution | *"Would having consistent, fresh estate-packed Assam CTC with moisture-proof packaging solve that?"* | *"Would automating 24/7 qualification with instant CRM handoff free up your closers?"* |

## 3. Objection Handling Protocol

When a customer raises an objection (e.g., price, delivery timeline, minimum order quantity, or onboarding complexity):
1. **Acknowledge and Validate**: Never argue or become defensive.
2. **Clarify the Root Concern**: Determine whether the objection is a budget constraint, a volume mismatch, or comparison against a lower-grade alternative.
3. **Provide Verified Evidence**: Reference verified specifications, ROI metrics, or policies stored in the **Knowledge Base (`/knowledge`)**.
4. **Suggest Low-Risk Trial**: Recommend a starter tier, commercial sample pack, or pilot milestone from the active catalog (`/products`).
